Common Tucson Pest Challenges

Subterranean Termites

Desert subterranean termites are prevalent across Pima County. Mud tubes on foundation walls, hollowed wood, or winged swarms after summer monsoon rains indicate active colonies requiring professional trenching or baiting.

Desert Scorpions & Spiders

Bark scorpions and black widows seek moisture during dry heat waves. Specialized residual perimeter treatments and blacklight inspections are critical to prevent indoor nesting.

Pack Rats & Rodents

Pack rats cause extensive attic, AC duct, and automotive wiring damage in the Sonoran desert. Effective control requires physical exclusion sealing plus targeted trapping.

General Desert Insects

Ants, roaches, and crickets multiply rapidly near irrigation lines and drains. Scheduled barrier treatments maintain long-term household defense.

Questions to Ask Before Hiring a Pest Specialist

  1. Are you licensed by the Arizona Department of Agriculture (PMD)? Verify applicator license numbers directly with state regulatory records.
  2. What specific treatment method do you propose? Ask whether bait stations, liquid termiticide barriers, or direct structural treatments are best suited for your home type.
  3. Is the inspection free, and is there a written warranty? Confirm treatment guarantees, re-treatment terms, and whether regular monitoring visits are included.
  4. What pet and child safety precautions are required? Ask how long family members and pets should stay away from treated interior surfaces.
